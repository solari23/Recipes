# Sriracha

## Ingredients
* 2 lbs red jalapeños (can mix in other red peppers)
* 3 cloves garlic
* 2 tbsp garlic powder
* 2 tbsp granulated sugar
* 1 tbsp light brown sugar
* 1 tbsp kosher salt

## Preparation
1. Remove stems from the jalapeños an cut in half lengthwise.
1. In a food processor (or blender) toss in all ingredients and pulse until they form a mash. (Like a chunky salsa).
1. Put mash in mason jar with pickling lid. Leave on counter for 7 days.
1. Every day, once per day, open jar and stir with a clean spoon.
1. After fermentation is done (after 7 days or when desired tanginess is reached), puree into sauce.

Store in fridge - will last many months.

Note: After a few days the mash and liquid will separate. This is normal. Daily stirring will re-incorporate.
